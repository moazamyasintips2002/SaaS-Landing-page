FAQ

Questions, answered

Still curious? Send us a note below — a human replies within one business day.

How does the 14-day trial work?

You get every Growth feature for 14 days with no card on file. When the trial ends you choose a plan or drop to the free workspace — nothing is charged automatically.

Can Cadence migrate our existing billing data?

Yes. Import subscriptions, invoices and price books from CSV or directly from Stripe, Chargebee or Paddle. Our team runs a free migration review for annual plans.

What counts as a metered event?

Any ingestion of usage data — an API call, a warehouse row or an SDK event. Aggregations, dashboards and alerts are always unlimited.

Do you support annual invoicing and purchase orders?

Scale plans support NET-30 invoices, PO numbers, multi-year terms and custom order forms alongside card and ACH payments.
